Ratatouille

* Servings: 6
* Prep Time: 30 mins
* Cook Time: 40 mins

Ingredients:

* 1 eggplant (dot not slot)
* 2 zucchinis (or squash)
* 3 bell peppers (yellow, orange and red)
* 1 small carton of sliced mushrooms
* 1 large can of diced tomatoes
* 1 onion
* garlic
* fresh thyme, rosemary and basil

Directions:

1. Peel and dice the vegetables (smaller is better)
2. Optional: roast each vegetable separately
3. Cook all the vegetables together, holding garlic and basil
4. Add the garlic and basil when close to cooked
