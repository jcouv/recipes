Gigot d'agneau

* Servings: 6
* Prep Time: 20 mins
* Cook Time: 

Ingredients:

* Shoulder of lamb
* 2 onions
* garlic
* potatoes

Directions:

1. trim some fat, then season the lamb with salt, pepper and garlic
2. peel the onions and cut in halves
3. dice the potatoes
4. load the lamb on bed of veggies in a large dish
5. cook for 20 minutes at 465F, then 390F until cooked (check internal temperature)
